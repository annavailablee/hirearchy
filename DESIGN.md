# Hirearchy — Design Notes

## Vibe

A warm, editorial-inspired SaaS dashboard that combines soft, pill-shaped modular widgets with rich, sophisticated earthy tones and expressive, playful interactions.

## Colors

* Primary: #4D0E12
* Secondary: #4A2E27
* Background: #F5EFC6
* Surface: #FFFFFF
* Text: #231815
* Muted: #A5BCD6
* Accent (success/match): #4D0E12
* Danger: #8B0000

## Dark or light?

Light only

## Typography

* Sans: Plus Jakarta Sans
* Mono: JetBrains Mono

## Animations

* Expressive (bouncy spring physics via Framer Motion, layout morphing, staggered card entrances, interactive magnetic buttons, animated ring loaders, and fluid page transitions)
